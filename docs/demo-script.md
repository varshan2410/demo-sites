# Two-minute demo script

1. Open the root route. Start with: “This is one configurable product system, not three unrelated sites.”
2. Open the clinic. Show the mobile navigation, theme toggle, doctor selection, appointment form and WhatsApp confirmation.
3. Show the smile comparison slider and print-ready confirmation.
4. Open the hotel. Switch the displayed currency, select a room, submit an enquiry and show the stay voucher.
5. Open the restaurant. Filter the menu, add two items, adjust a quantity and open the generated WhatsApp order.
6. Make a table reservation and point out that the three flows are purpose-built while the shell and config system are reused.
7. End at the showcase page and briefly open config/hotel.ts to show that a brand/content change does not require component changes.

## Suggested closing line

“I prioritized the mobile sales experience and reusable configuration system. Booking, availability and payment behaviour are intentionally demo-grade, with limitations documented rather than hidden.”
